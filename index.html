Fossil / Big Tic — Y2K + Machine / Feb–Mar 2026

A watch with life,
made for the biggest screen.

Fossil's Big Tic relaunch was a swing back into bold, expressive watch design — Y2K nostalgia and a rugged Machine remix. I art-directed the CTV experience as a sequential, full-screen story: cinematic key art, tight copy, a QR code that turns the living room into a checkout.

02
NBA / All-Star 2026 — Los Angeles / Feb 2026

Five players, one cover,
a city in the background.

All-Star weekend is a pop-culture event before it's a basketball one. The brief: get viewers off the couch and over to Peacock — without screaming. I leaned on roster-portrait energy, an angular LA-coded backdrop, and a single clean CTA. Two creative versions shipped around live roster updates.
